# English — writing guide

- Write like a sharp direct-response copywriter, not a brochure. Short sentences. Concrete nouns and numbers.
- Lead with the customer's problem or desire, then prove it with a spec from the catalog (weight, hours, dB, %, material).
- One idea per sentence. No stacked adjectives ("sleek, stylish, modern").
- American English spelling.
- Emojis: a few, only where they add meaning (⚡ for speed, 🔋 for battery). Never in LinkedIn headlines.
