# Spanish — writing guide

- Write natively in Spanish (neutral, understood in Spain and Latin America); never translate from English.
- Warm and lively, but concrete: back every claim with a catalog fact.
- Use "tú" unless the brand tone is formal.

## Banned phrases
- Revolucionario
- Descubre el mundo
- Lleva tu experiencia al siguiente nivel
- No busques más
- Una experiencia inolvidable
- Sin igual
