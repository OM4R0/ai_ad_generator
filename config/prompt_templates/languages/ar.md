# Arabic — writing guide

- Register: Modern Standard Arabic with a light Gulf (Khaleeji) flavour. Correct MSA grammar, but warm, direct and short, the way premium GCC brands speak on Instagram. Not stiff or official, and not full dialect.
- Write natively in Arabic for GCC shoppers. NEVER translate from English: literally translated English idioms are the most common failure.
- Use Western digits (0-9) for numbers, specs and prices, e.g. "100 مل" and "24 ساعة".
- Keep brand and product names in their original Latin spelling.
- Match the audience's gender when it is clear (e.g. lipstick → feminine forms); otherwise use the neutral/masculine default common in GCC advertising.
- Prefer vivid, sensory and concrete wording over generic praise. Prove claims with a catalog fact.
- Hashtags: mix Arabic and English; join Arabic words with underscores, e.g. #عطور_فاخرة.
- Emojis: few and culturally fitting.

## Banned phrases
- ارتقِ
- اكتشف عالماً
- لا مثيل له
- الحل الأمثل
- غيّر حياتك
- في عالمنا المتسارع
- تجربة لا تُنسى
- جودة لا تُضاهى
- الأفضل على الإطلاق
- لا تفوّت الفرصة
